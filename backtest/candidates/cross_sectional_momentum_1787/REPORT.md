## Limits

- Execution surfaces not modeled: maker fills, a separate spread, order-book depth and market impact, intrabar execution, venue outages and partial venue fills (every scripted fill is a full taker fill at the step mark plus slippage), and historical changes of szDecimals or minimum order size (lot metadata is read once at preparation).
- Marks are the open of the daily Hyperliquid bar at each step; the venue's mark and oracle prices, which drive funding and liquidation, can differ from candle prices. Funding is charged from the venue's hourly rates at the sampled step mark.
- Members hold separate paper cash; idle cash earns nothing. Returns are stated on the gross budget, and the summed member capital is larger than the gross budget.
- The candidate's target net weight is exactly 0, but a dollar-neutral basket is not beta neutral, and transitions pass through states bounded by the candidate's own net allowance; baselines declare a larger net allowance for their long or directional holdings.
- The universe is a fixed set of surviving large coins chosen by a frozen rule; the result does not cover delisted coins, new listings or a larger universe.
- A study pass would grant no live admission; the basket live refusal stays in force for every outcome.
