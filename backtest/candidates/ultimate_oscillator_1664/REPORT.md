## Protocol (frozen in `study_spec.json` before held-out results were read)

- Candidate `ultimate_oscillator`, direction `both`, close owner `tiered_tp_pct` (one tier: 3% profit closes the full position), sole stop owner fixed entry ATR x1.0, strict close comparison, `ohlc_walk` intrabar policy, capital $1000 per leg, next-bar-open fills.
- Selection on `is` over the 729-combination optimizer grid: eligible combinations score all six datasets, have no liquidated leg and at least 30 positions on the selection window; pick the highest mean Sharpe, then the highest mean DD-adjusted return, then the earliest grid order.
- Paired control: `donchian_breakout` with entry_period = breakout_lookback, exit_period 10 (unused: the close strategy and stop own every exit); entries zeroed where the candidate uo_valid is false. The same close, stop, direction, capital, costs and data apply.
- Primary comparisons: the M1 incumbent bar (median of 8 incumbents per window and dataset; incumbents run long-only without a close strategy) and the masked control.
- Sample rule: the pooled held-out result for the selected parameters needs at least 30 independent position clusters and at least 10 long and 10 short positions. A cluster merges positions of the same side in the same window whose holding intervals overlap on any dataset, so the 1h and 4h legs of one asset and simultaneous positions across assets count once.
- Verdict rule: inconclusive when held-out candle or funding coverage is incomplete (candidate, stress, masked control or incumbent legs), when a leg is missing, when the sample rule fails, or when no neighbouring variant is evaluable. Pass only when there is no liquidation, pooled net PnL is positive at base cost and with doubled slippage, the pooled mean Sharpe and mean DD-adjusted return beat both the fixed-incumbent median bar and the masked Donchian control, and at least two thirds of the evaluable one-step grid neighbours are net positive and beat their own masked control on both metrics. Any other fully evaluated result is fail.
