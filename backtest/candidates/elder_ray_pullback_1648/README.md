# Elder Ray pullback entries (#1648)

Research candidate `elder_ray_pullback`. It is registered for the futures registry only, it declares
`short_entries=True`, and it carries `edge_status="no_edge"`. Profitability is not known. This study gives
retrospective evidence only. It does not change admission, live defaults or deployed configuration.

The specification below, `candidate_seed.json` and `backtest/candidates/elder_ray_pullback_1648_manifest.json`
were frozen before any held-out result was read. `REPORT.md` and `results.json` are generated by `run_study.py`.

## Signal specification

Formula reference: Bull Bear Power (Elder Ray). The external page motivates the candidate and supplies no
Hyperliquid edge evidence.

**Finite exponential moving average (EMA).** For every timestamp `t` the average `E(t)` reads exactly the
64 closes `close[t-63] .. close[t]`:

```
alpha = 2 / (ema_period + 1)
e = close[t-63]
for k in t-62 .. t:   e = alpha * close[k] + (1 - alpha) * e
E(t) = e
```

Each earlier average `E(t-j)` is computed independently by the same rule from its own 64 closes. An
unrestricted EMA starts once at the first input row and keeps every older close with a geometrically
smaller weight. That makes its value depend on where the input frame starts. The finite EMA forgets every
close older than 64 bars, so `E(t)` is the same in the full series, in every prefix and in any bounded
window that contains those 64 closes. The seed close keeps the weight `(1 - alpha)^63`: about 0.0195 at
`ema_period=32`, 0.0025 at 21 and 0.00006 at 13. The finite EMA therefore differs from an unrestricted EMA,
most at the largest period. That difference is part of the specification.

**Slope and powers.**

- `S(t) = E(t) - E(t - slope_span)` in price units.
- Bull power `BU(t) = high(t) - E(t)`. Bear power `BE(t) = low(t) - E(t)`.

**Setups and entries.** All comparisons are strict unless written otherwise.

- Long condition `L(t)`: `S(t) > 0` and `BE(t) < 0`. Short condition `H(t)`: `S(t) < 0` and `BU(t) > 0`.
- A long setup arms at bar `a` when `L(a)` is true and `L(a-1)` is false. Its age at bar `t` is `t - a`.
  While `L` stays true the setup keeps its arming bar. The same excursion never rearms.
- Long entry on bar `t`: `L(t-1)` is true, `S(t) > 0`, `BE(t) >= 0` (a zero recovery counts) and the age
  `t - a <= pullback_window`. Age equal to `pullback_window` is eligible; a larger age is expired.
- Short entry mirrors it: `H(t-1)` true, `S(t) < 0`, `BU(t) <= 0`, age `<= pullback_window`.
- A zero or opposite slope while a setup is live ends the setup without an entry (`trend_cleared`). A later
  bar where the condition becomes true after a false bar arms a new setup.
- The recovery bar ends the run, so one setup gives at most one entry. A new entry needs a new pullback.
- `L` and `H` cannot be true on the same bar, and a long and a short entry cannot occur on the same bar.

**Dependency span.** A decision on bar `t` reads `L` and `H` on bars `t - pullback_window - 1 .. t`. Each of
those needs `E` at that bar and `slope_span` bars earlier, and each `E` needs 64 closes. The span is
`64 + slope_span + pullback_window + 1` rows. At the domain bounds (`slope_span=8`, `pullback_window=16`) it
is 89 rows, below the 200-row check window and the existing feed lookback, so no runtime argument, probe or
feed change is needed.

**Validity.** A decision is made only when every row of its own span has finite open, high, low, close and
volume, positive prices, `low <= open, close <= high`, volume `>= 0`, strictly increasing timestamps with one
constant spacing, and every computed `E`, `S`, `BU` and `BE` on bars `t - pullback_window - 1 .. t` is
finite. Otherwise the bar holds with a reason code. A defect holds entries for one full span after it and
cannot change any decision outside that span. After the span clears, a setup needs a fresh arming bar
inside the clean span.

**Reason codes** (`erp_reason`, `erp_reason_code`): `entry_long` 1, `entry_short` -1, `no_setup` 0,
`pullback_long` 2, `pullback_short` -2 (a live setup, with `erp_setup_side` and `erp_setup_age`),
`setup_expired` 3, `trend_cleared` 4, `insufficient_history` 10, `missing_columns` 11,
`missing_timestamps` 12, `nonfinite_input` 13, `nonpositive_price` 14, `invalid_candle_geometry` 15,
`negative_volume` 16, `timestamp_order` 17, `irregular_cadence` 18, `arithmetic_overflow` 19.

**Recorded values.** Every row carries `erp_eval_ts_ms`, `erp_high`, `erp_low`, `erp_close`, `erp_ema`,
`erp_ema_prior`, `erp_slope`, `erp_bull_power`, `erp_bear_power`, `erp_setup_side`, `erp_setup_age`, the
validity flags `erp_ema_valid`, `erp_slope_valid`, `erp_valid`, the parameters and the required history. An
invalid value is written as 0 with its flag at 0. `check_hyperliquid.py` reports every `erp_` value at full
float precision and `erp_reason` as an indicator label.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Default | Domain | Optimizer grid |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ema_period` | 13 | integer 2..32 | 8, 13, 21 |
| `slope_span` | 3 | integer 1..8 | 1, 3, 5 |
| `pullback_window` | 8 | integer 1..16 | 4, 8, 16 |

Booleans, strings, fractions, non-finite values and out-of-domain values are refused by the registry
`param_validator` before any market fetch (solo checks, batch slots, `validate_params`). Integral floats such
as `13.0` are accepted as the integer.

## Study protocol (frozen)

- Data: the hash-pinned Hyperliquid 4h candles, hourly funding and venue references under
  `backtest/candidates/connors_rsi_reversion_1645/data/`, referenced in place by the manifest. Every sha256
  is verified on load.
- Windows: train [2024-09-01, 2025-09-01), test [2025-09-01, 2026-09-01), 300 warm-up bars. Earlier studies
  used the same windows, so this is retrospective evidence.
- Close owner `tiered_tp_pct` with its default ladder, comparison mode strict, one fixed entry-ATR stop at
  x1.0, capital $1000. A bar N signal fills at the bar N+1 open.
- Arms: `elder_ray_pullback` (both), `momentum_pro` (both, an M1 incumbent), `bear_pullback_st` (short, its
  native direction; not an M1 incumbent). Every arm uses the same close, stop, capital, windows and costs.
  Comparator implementations and registry defaults are unchanged.
- The M1 incumbents are scored under the matched incumbent contract (`incumbent_contract` in the seed) as a
  reference beside the verdict.
- Funding: issue 1731 is complete (pull request 1745). Every leg uses the existing charge-mode funding
  attachment. A coverage gate checks the exact candle grid, warm-up, left-closed funding hours and the
  right-closed hourly accrual span before any scoring; a refusal makes the verdict inconclusive. Missing
  funding is never treated as zero.
- Costs: the manifest execution specification (taker 0.045%, maker 0.015%, 5 bps slippage per side plus the
  per-coin half spread, szDecimals lot flooring, $10 minimum order with a 3% margin). Stress doubles spread
  and slippage. The half spread and venue metadata are current pinned snapshots applied to historical
  candles. No proxy candles are used.
- Selection on train only: eligible combos have complete coverage, every dataset scored, no liquidated leg
  and at least 10 independent positions; order by mean net Sharpe, then mean drawdown-adjusted return, then
  grid order.
- Held-out gates: at least 30 independent positions for every selected arm; the candidate needs both long
  and short positions. Missing coverage or sample support gives inconclusive.
- Pass: candidate net PnL above 0 at base and stress cost, and candidate base mean Sharpe and mean
  drawdown-adjusted return strictly above both selected comparators. A complete study that misses any pass
  check is a fail.
- Descriptive outputs (never verdict inputs): defaults, unsuccessful variants, turnover, independent
  counts, long and short net PnL, concentration, neighbour stability, train vs held-out rank correlation and
  a seeded bootstrap interval.

## Limits

- The held-out window was used by earlier studies; the result is retrospective.
- Positions overlap in time; the bootstrap interval is optimistic.
- Queue position, partial fills, depth beyond the frozen half spread and venue outages are not modeled.
- A pass would still leave the strategy `no_edge`. Any live use needs a separate reviewed operator action
  under the existing acknowledgement rule.
